Why Sarhad

Frontier AI wasn't built for what you can't share.

The best AI models live outside your walls. The data that would make them useful — account numbers, identity numbers, client names, contract terms — can't go with them. Today that leaves most organizations choosing between capability and control.

Sarhad removes the choice. It keeps the sensitive part of the document at home, and sends the model everything it needs to reason well regardless.

How It Works

One border. Two sides.

01

Detect

A trained model scans every document for sensitive identifiers, layered with deterministic rules for the formats general models miss.

02

Mask

Each identifier is replaced with a stable, reversible placeholder. The model sees structure and context — never the real value.

03

Process

Your frontier model of choice reasons over the masked document exactly as it would over the original — same quality, same output shape.

04

Restore

Real values are restored only inside your own infrastructure — never inside the AI provider's, and never logged outside your walls.
